# References for papers shown in the current TVC plots

The figures contain eight distinct publications. A paper that appears in more than one plot is listed only once here. “Our Vehicle” is not a publication. Wilson and Riccardi (2022), QuadRocket, EAGLE, and the other contextual papers discussed in the reading guide are not plotted and are therefore not included in this list.
